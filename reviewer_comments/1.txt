Comments to Authors of “Towards single-shot coherent imaging via overlap-free ptychography”
Dear authors,
I believe that there is a large amount of technical content in your manuscript “Towards singleshot coherent imaging via overlap-free ptychography” but the current state of the manuscript
does not provide the readers with a self-contained presentation.
A few things stand out when one reads the manuscript. First of all, the presentation quality
degrades considerably past the “Introduction” section as the authors resolve to a bullet point-like
format for their presentation. After reading the manuscript a few times, I am not clear on whether
single-shot reconstructions with PtychoPINN are presented in a visual format. Does Figure 1
present the single-shot reconstructions using PtychoPINN? There are many places where
technical discussions are presented without prior introduction or explanation. For example, it is
unclear to me why the authors offer a discussion on the “extended probe” under the “Neural
Network Architecture” subsection. In the meantime, I would like to point out that although the
paper serves as an extension to the Scientific Reports paper by Hoidn et al. (2023), it is currently
formulated like an addendum to the previous work rather than a self-contained article. For the
general audience of Optica, it will be important to re-introduce the architecture of the ML
algorithm with the re-parametrization of overlap constraint instead of simply referring to your
previous work. In addition, Figure 2 suffers from small fonts as well as insufficient explanation –
what is the Tike reconstruction?
I hope that my review does not come across as overly critical of the manuscript. Again, I think
that the technical content of the manuscript is high yet the communication of such technical
content is lacking.
Best regards,
Reviewer
One additional question: in the PtychoPINN reconstructions, are probes solved for as unknown variables or are ground truth supplied?
