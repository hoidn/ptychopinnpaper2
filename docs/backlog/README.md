# Paper Revision Backlog

This directory tracks paper-revision backlog items that are not complete until the paper is updated end to end.

For each item, completion requires:

- generating or revising the relevant figure, table, data file, or manuscript text;
- compiling the paper and visually inspecting the affected pages in `ptychopinn_2025.pdf`;
- iterating on layout, formatting, caption clarity, and aesthetics until the paper-ready result is acceptable;
- updating `reviewer_revision_checklist.md` for every completed reviewer-revision item, including verification-only items;
- updating `changelog.txt` when reviewer-facing manuscript, table, figure, or data changes are made, or recording in the backlog item why no changelog entry was needed;
- recording the source artifacts, commands, and any decisions or pivots in the backlog item or linked notes.
